# 0005: Evidence-bound, review-only benchmark reconciliation

Benchmark comparisons are immutable PostgreSQL records, not new semantic primitives or authority
events. Domain articles take precedence over workbook projections; all original snapshots survive.
Publisher edition succession requires an explicit quoted passage. Control-level similarity and
SemVer classifications remain proposals, independently of publication maturity and enterprise use.

Candidate retrieval combines explicit namespaced identity, structural similarity and optional
domain/scoped embeddings. Embeddings never establish equivalence. A separately configured small
model can verify meaning; its schema and evidence must validate against both exact source passages.
Offline structural results are labeled as such. Unknown numeric version baselines stay unknown.

Published `mapsTo` records preserve source-specific identifiers, revisions, coverage and passages.
Native resolution uses exact configured framework/revision and publisher OSCAL label aliases,
including actual enhancement labels rather than guessed machine-ID transformations. Native clause
identities are reference anchors, not assertions of per-clause coverage or compliance.

Comparisons, vectors and valid judgments have distinct replay keys. Parsing/revision changes retain
new evidence without unnecessarily repeating model calls for unchanged semantic inputs. Provider
calls occur outside database transactions. Read-only inspection may produce an explicitly labeled
structural preview, but never calls providers or persists business proposals.
